    Cauldron

    It's Halloween night in the cottage, and the potion book is open. Each recipe card asks for a potion: put in the ingredients in order, the right number of each, then stir.

    • Click a jar on the shelf, or a button below, to drop it in. Keys 1–8 work too. S or Space stirs, P pours out, Enter goes on, M mutes.
    • Tap a jar on the shelf, or a button below, to drop it in. Then tap Stir!
    • Get it wrong and something silly happens. Pour it out and try again: there's no hurry.
    • The last three recipes are riddles: work out the ingredients from the clues.

    A make-believe cottage with make-believe magic. Built on the Strata engine; source.
